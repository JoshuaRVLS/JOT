### 2. jot-light fails AA on 26 text pairings, jot-dark on 1 (R-25, R-34)

Computed per theme group against the background that group declares, which is
the pairing the painter uses. Terminal text is normal size, so the bar is
4.5:1.

| 2.74 | StatusLineMuted, TabLine, TerminalTab | `#9b8b96` on `#fbe9e7` / `#fdf1f2` |

jot-dark: LineNr `#655e8c` on `#131024` at 3.13:1, and nothing else.

The Comment row is the one that reaches the settings panel: the section
heading, the search placeholder, the match count and the steppers all paint
in `Comment` on the panel background. The single-value fix already agreed
for that row is `#8d7f8b` to `#7a6b78`, which computes to 4.82:1. The other
25 rows are a palette tune, not a one-liner: they are the light theme's
mid-tones sitting just under the line, so darkening them shifts the
theme's whole character.

This is measured off the screen, not off the file: running the editor with
`color_scheme=jot-light` emits `38;2;141;127;139` (that `Comment` ink) and
never the dark theme's `38;2;139;131;176`, and the dark run is the mirror of
that. So the pairing above is what a light-mode user is looking at.

### 4. The settings panel is 60 cells wide in a 30-cell terminal (R-03, R-35)

The width is `min(max(60, screen_w - 12), 96)`, so the floor of 60 wins on
any terminal narrower than 72 columns. A pty run at 44x14 and at 30x10,
`:settings` open:

```
=== 44x14 ===                       === 30x10 ===
|┌  Settings              ..─       |┌  Settings        ..─
|│ ? Search 107 settings  ..         |│ ? Search 107 ...  ..
|│──────────────────────..           |│──────────────────..
|│Auto-detect indent off ..          |│Auto-detect ind..  ..
```

The right border, the divider and every value are clipped (`..` is the
renderer's own clip marker), and the value column never fits at all. The
panel's height already adapts (`min(20, max(9, screen_h - 10))`); only the
width has a floor above the screen.

Fix direction: make the floor the screen, not 60, and let `key_w` shrink
with it, then re-run this same dump at 30 and 44 columns.

| Theme | Group | Ratio |

written down in an earlier change. Recorded so the choice stays explicit.

### 8. Purpose test on the new panel affordances (R-04, R-06, R-31)

Filing the reasons, since the rule is that a technique is allowed once its
purpose is written:

- section headings in the theme's `Comment` ink: they name a group, and the
  quiet ink is what keeps them from competing with the rows under them;
- `nf-fa-minus` / `nf-fa-plus` on an int row: a stepper says which direction
  it moves the number, unlike a neutral dot;
- `nf-fa-check` in the choices list: marks the value in force, which the row
  itself cannot show while the list covers it;
- single guillemets around an enum's choice: the choice cycles, and nothing
  in the row's text says so;
- no sparkle, robot, lightning or orb anywhere in the surface.
